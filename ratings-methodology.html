Layer-by-layer ratings methodology.

Looking through the token to the yield source underneath.

Kurtosis rates on-chain fixed income positions across their full economic chain: the held asset, the wrapper protocol, how you exit, and what each depends on. The current research edition contains no approved scores or grades. The v0.3-alpha methodology is a draft; evidence and unresolved approval requirements remain visible.

Explore Research Assessments Download Full Methodology
1

Economic Exposure & Asset Quality

Deconstructs the yield generation engine beneath the token wrapper. Evaluates collateral backing, credit quality, seniority, cash flow stability, and first-loss junior absorption limits.

  • Senior vs. junior loss absorption verification
  • Underlying collateral weights and dated holdings
  • Net Asset Value (NAV) sync and impairment recognition
2

Protocol & Wrapper Integrity

Evaluates smart contract logic, yield stripping and principal token tokenization, account segregation terms, and deployed binaries against matched security audits.

  • Vault wrapper conversion rate verification
  • Timelock and upgrade authority inspection
  • Independent code audit reconciliation
3

Measured Exit Routes

Tests the realization path back to clean settlement currency (USDC). Distinguishes between theoretical liquidity figures and measured, executable market depth at volume.

  • Maturity payoff and redemption path simulation
  • Stressed secondary market quotes and slippage
  • Withdrawal pause and queue settlement mechanics
4

Shared Controls & Governance

Identifies single points of failure, administrative keys, multi-sig thresholds, oracle price feeds, and legal rights under applicable insolvency frameworks.

  • Signed participation terms and legal priority
  • Oracle data stream dependency checks
  • Cross-layer administrative power mapping

How Ratings Inform Horizon Vaults

Ratings are designed to inform qualification and position risk controls. The current assessments remain NO_RATE and do not establish allocation approval. Horizon is an illustrative product preview.
