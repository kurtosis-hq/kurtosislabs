01.Logo
Kurtosis Labs

The mark is a letter K cut into three clean planes: a tall column with a sloped foot and two triangles, held apart by a narrow gap. It stands for the work itself, a complex position taken apart into its layers so each one can be seen, measured and underwritten. The wordmark is set in Helvetica Neue Medium.

Three planes

A column and two triangles form one K.

Layered gaps

The gaps read as separate layers, the Russian Doll idea.

Soft corners

Rounded corners keep the exact geometry approachable at any size.

02.Clear Space
X = ¼ height
of the mark

Keep clear space of X on all four sides of the mark. No type, image edge or other graphic may enter this area. Smallest size: 16 px tall on screen, 6 mm in print.

Solid navy chrome

Each coin is polished navy metal with a raised rim, a finely engraved face and a raised white-silver logo. A blue reflection grows toward the edges.

Ring of ten

Ten coins sit in a tilted ring, leaning like turbine blades, each turning slowly while the ring circles anticlockwise. Logos always stay upright.

On light grounds

Place coins on white or the sky tint with a soft shadow below. Never make them see-through, recolour them or crop a coin's face.

Icon coins · finance set

Same coin, icon inlay

The navy chrome coin with a white line icon set into the face in place of a token logo. Lines are even and rounded, like the Lucide icons in 06.

Where to use them

Feature lists, product pages, decks and social posts: one coin per idea, at one size in a row.

Keep it to one icon

One simple symbol per coin, always white, always upright. Don't add text to the face.

08.Photography

City skylines at blue hour

Financial-district waterfronts at dusk or night: deep blue sky, lit towers, long reflections in calm water.

Navy wash under text

Whenever text sits on a photo, lay #010813 at 69% over it so white type stays readable.

Rounded frames

Framed photos take 25 px corners. Avoid people, logos of other firms and daylight scenes.

10.Motion

Calm, in order

Blocks rise 90 px and sharpen from a light blur; their words then drift up one after another. Things that arrive together appear top to bottom, a beat apart.

Hold, then step

Sections with several points hold the page still and change one point per scroll, so each point is read before the page moves on.

Metal, not cartoon

Coins are solid navy chrome with a blue edge. They move along smooth curves and settle into a slow turn; nothing bounces or flashes.
