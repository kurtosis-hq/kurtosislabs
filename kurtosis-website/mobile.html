Layers hide what you own

One asset can be wrapped, deposited into a yield protocol, split into principal and yield tokens and used as collateral elsewhere. Each step changes what can go wrong.

02

A good month can hide a problem

A market-neutral portfolio can make additional profits from an unknown interest-rate or credit exposure. The profit does not make that exposure acceptable.

03

Things change while you sleep

An exploit, a depeg or a liquidation can happen overnight. Checking X and Telegram is not a monitoring process.
