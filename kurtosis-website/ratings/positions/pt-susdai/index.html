Scenario assumption

B assumes present capacity with working withdrawals, but assumes a thinner liquidity and net-recovery cushion under the common stress than the other scenario cases.

Documented risk

Illiquid GPU loans expose the matured PT to borrower and recovery risk. Withdrawals depend on cash after loan commitments and can carry into later epochs.

Supporting observations

Reviewed 25 October: exact matured linkage, three sampled transfers, reserve arithmetic and conditional quotes. These do not establish full loan performance or cash settlement.

Still unknown

Current servicing, NAV, recovery rights, all-chain liabilities, cash after commitments, queue servicing and complete funded PT-to-USDC execution remain unverified.

Assumptions needed for B

  1. 01

    Assume a current reconciled loan and servicing tape demonstrates performing loans, realisable economic NAV and present capacity. Historical inspections and a few repayment transfers do not establish this assumption.

    Unverified
  2. 02

    Assume enforceable holder-to-recovery rights, realistic net collateral recoveries and reconciled all-chain reserves, liabilities and restrictions. Do not use the separate USDai/PYUSD reserve branch to offset GPU-loan exposure without evidence.

    Unverified
  3. 03

    Assume the exact matured PT payoff is reconciled and cash after commitments, eligible queue servicing and a complete funded native-USDC route work within the common size and timing comparison. No privileged access, immediate par exit or guaranteed epoch payment is assumed.

    Unverified
  4. 04

    Assume a thinner stressed liquidity and net-recovery cushion than the ONyc reference case because delayed collections, commitments and recovery costs absorb more available resources. This relative weakness is hypothetical; GPU lending is not automatically ranked below insurance.
