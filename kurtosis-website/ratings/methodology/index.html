All four positions: NR, insufficient basis for a grade. Missing evidence does not prove distress, and working mechanisms alone do not prove payment capacity. Evidence confidence remains Low; calibration and independent model review are still pending.

Four evidence gates

All four must be supported before any grade. Today none is supported for any position.

Gate 1

Holder rights

An enforceable claim and recovery priority.

Gate 2

Backing

The assets and reserves, and how they absorb losses.

Gate 3

Token payoff

The exact instrument and its conversion mechanics.

Gate 4

Cash access

Each step from the holding to USDC.

Scenario gradesConditional cases

A scenario grade is a conditional letter: if the stated assumptions held, this is how resilient the position would be under a common stress. It is not a rating, not a forecast and not the most likely outcome. The evidence rating stays NR for every position.

Common stress: material asset losses and delayed collections, combined with an exit-liquidity squeeze and no new funding. Reference position: US$1 million at reported NAV (net asset value), unlevered, aiming to reach native USDC within 30 days.

How to read the scale
Scale
AAA, AA, A, BBB, BB, B, CCC, CC, C, D, with plus and minus modifiers. Only BB, B and CCC are defined categories so far.
What each grade needs
BB needs supported, stronger speculative-grade resilience. B needs evidenced current capacity to meet the relevant obligations. CCC needs evidenced present vulnerability.
NR
Not rated: insufficient basis for a grade. It is neither a safe rating nor a distress rating.
A corrected error
An earlier version gave CCC to positions with unverified evidence. That treated missing information as distress, which was a methodology error. Missing evidence now earns NR, never CCC. Removing CCC is a correction, not an upgrade.
Notation
The scale borrows S&P's published notation as a reference only. These are Kurtosis judgements, not ratings issued or endorsed by S&P or any rating agency.
Why each grade is withheld
  • ONycNR · Low evidence confidence
    Documented risk
    Insurance claims and reserve revisions can reduce value. Open-access terms disclaim contractual rights against the issuer for that path; cash access remains conditional.
    Supporting observations
    Reviewed 25 October: successful USDG quote simulations and observed balances support a possible exit route, not executed cash settlement or reserve adequacy.
    Still unknown
    Current claims, reserves and NAV need independent reconciliation. Holder rights, cash after commitments and a funded native-USDC exit remain unverified.
    Why not CCC
    The reviewed record does not establish present vulnerability or dependence on favourable conditions. Missing reports and one unavailable direct USDC pair do not demonstrate the present financial vulnerability required for CCC.
    Why not B
    B would assert present capacity to meet a defined commitment. Successful quotes do not establish that capacity without reconciled claims, usable assets and holder rights; BB would need stronger evidence of resilience.
  • PT-ONycNR · Low evidence confidence
    Documented risk
    The matured September PT still inherits ONyc’s insurance, rights and liquidity risks. Converting to ONyc is an intermediate step, not cash.
    Supporting observations
    Reviewed 25 October: exact PT, vault and maturity matched on-chain. Underlying USDG quotes suggest a possible route but do not validate PT-to-USDC execution.
    Still unknown
    ONyc backing and holder rights remain unresolved. This PT’s supply, payoff and complete funded cash route need reconciliation and validation.
    Why not CCC
    Remaining supply after maturity does not establish nonpayment, and a small unexplained counter difference is not a measured shortfall. Neither establishes present vulnerability of this claim or dependence on favourable conditions.
    Why not B
    Exact identity and elapsed maturity do not prove present payment capacity. B still needs an enforceable claim, reconciled ONyc backing and verified PT proceeds; a wrapper alone cannot support BB or an uplift.
  • PT-srONycNR · Low evidence confidence
    Documented risk
    Junior capital absorbs losses first, but protection is finite. Senior withdrawals can pause during recovery; the matured PT retains ONyc asset and rights risks.
    Supporting observations
    Reviewed 25 October: Active tranche, no recorded losses and two historical PT-to-senior-token conversions. These do not verify usable protection or cash settlement.
    Still unknown
    Usable junior protection under stress, ONyc backing and rights, exact PT payoff, recovery access and a funded cash exit remain unverified.
    Why not CCC
    The reviewed record does not establish present vulnerability of this senior claim or dependence on favourable conditions. Active state and conversion receipts show limited operation, not independently assessed payment capacity or stress resilience.
    Why not B
    B would require demonstrated present capacity after the underlying and wrapper risks are considered. An observed junior balance and senior label do not prove realisable protection, legal recourse or cash delivery; BB would need verified stress resilience.
  • PT-sUSDaiNR · Low evidence confidence
    Documented risk
    Illiquid GPU loans expose the matured PT to borrower and recovery risk. Withdrawals depend on cash after loan commitments and can carry into later epochs.
    Supporting observations
    Reviewed 25 October: exact matured linkage, three sampled transfers, reserve arithmetic and conditional quotes. These do not establish full loan performance or cash settlement.
    Still unknown
    Current servicing, NAV, recovery rights, all-chain liabilities, cash after commitments, queue servicing and complete funded PT-to-USDC execution remain unverified.
    Why not CCC
    The reviewed record does not establish present vulnerability or dependence on favourable conditions. Quote dispersion and a cash-dependent queue show exposure and execution uncertainty, not by themselves the vulnerability required for CCC.
    Why not B
    B would require affirmative evidence of present capacity. Historical loan inspections, three transfer samples and unexecuted quotes cannot establish current portfolio collectability or cash available to this holder; BB would need stronger stress support.
From evidence to an approved rating
  1. 01

    Define the exact position

    Network, contract or mint, wrapper, series, maturity, holder access and position size.

  2. 02

    Score four components

    Instrument, claims, substance and venue.

  3. 03

    Trace each risk once

    Use the most adverse factor, with no averaging and no automatic credit for buffers.

  4. 04

    Validate before release

    Reconcile, test alternatives, get external review, then record a dated committee release decision.

How the calculation works
Factor anchors
0, 0.5 and 1, where higher means more modelled risk.
Missing evidence
Leaves a blank and blocks approval. It is never filled with an assumed value.
Separate decisions
The economic score, the rating, evidence confidence, watchlist flags and release blocks are decided separately.
Confidence cap
Confidence is capped at Moderate until the rubric is calibrated. There are zero matched factor-to-outcome observations so far.
Evidence states
Supported, Partial, Conflicting, Missing, Unavailable, Stale and N/A describe individual requirements only. They are not a completion percentage or a confidence score.
Dates, quotes and updates
Evidence dates
Each assessment keeps its own cutoff date. Refreshing the page does not refresh the evidence.
Quotes
A quote simulation is not an executed trade. No transaction was simulated or submitted for any exit route.
Live updates
Reported developments prompt a review only. They never change a grade, confidence or status automatically. Checks run at most every five minutes while a page is visible.
Times
All times are shown in UTC.
Glossary
NR
Not rated. Insufficient basis for a grade; it establishes neither safety nor distress.
PT (principal token)
A token for a specific maturity. Maturity alone does not establish payment in cash; check the exact assets and exit route.
Live updates
Reported developments shown separately from the dated assessment.
Downloads and private notes
Assessment and source records download as JSON; reports as Markdown or plain text. Your notes stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
