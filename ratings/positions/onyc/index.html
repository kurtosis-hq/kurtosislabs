Scenario assumption

BB- assumes a speculative case with present capacity and some stress resilience, if current insurance backing, holder rights and cash access are established.

Documented risk

Insurance claims and reserve revisions can reduce value. Open-access terms disclaim contractual rights against the issuer for that path; cash access remains conditional.

Supporting observations

Reviewed 25 October: successful USDG quote simulations and observed balances support a possible exit route, not executed cash settlement or reserve adequacy.

Still unknown

Current claims, reserves and NAV need independent reconciliation. Holder rights, cash after commitments and a funded native-USDC exit remain unverified.

Assumptions needed for BB-

  1. 01

    Assume current treaty assets, claims, reserves, commitments and economic NAV are reconciled and show present capacity with some resilience under the common stress. This has not been established from the available record.

    Assume the ordinary secondary holder has documented, enforceable claim and transfer rights applicable to this exact acquisition path, despite the unresolved open-access disclaimers. Token ownership alone does not establish those rights; no privileged issuer access is assumed.

    Unverified
